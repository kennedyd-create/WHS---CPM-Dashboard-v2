📖 Eat Your Ice Cream: Six Simple Rules for a Long & Healthy Life

Dr. Ezekiel J. Emanuel — oncologist, bioethicist, and health policy expert

The book is a grounded, evidence-based antidote to what the author calls the "wellness industrial complex" — the billion-dollar industry of biohacks, rigid diets, unproven longevity fads, and obsessive tracking. Emanuel's core argument: health should be an invisible, effortless background habit, not a stressful full-time job, and a long life is only valuable if it's joyful and meaningful.

Key Themes & Takeaways

  • Wellness is a means, not the end. Living longer isn't a competition — the goal of health is simply to give you the vitality to enjoy your life, foster relationships, and engage with your community.
  • The metaphor behind the title. "Eat your ice cream" is a call for balance over extreme deprivation. Joy, social connection, and mental satisfaction play a massive role in longevity — sometimes even more than hyper-strict physical habits.
  • 6 evidence-based pillars, distilled from decades of medical research:
    1. Nutrition — real food, less processed sugar, shared meals
    2. Exercise — consistency through enjoyable movement over extreme workouts
    3. Sleep — regular rest without obsessive gadget tracking
    4. Alcohol — sensible limits rather than panic
    5. Mental Acuity — lifelong curiosity and learning
    6. Social Connection — one of the strongest indicators of long-term health

"Life is not a competition to live the longest, and wellness should not be a full-time job."

A practical, common-sense roadmap to living well — without letting the pursuit of perfect health consume your happiness.

🏫 Turning the AI's output into a Doc

The AI will return a block of HTML code. Here's the quickest way to turn it into a real, formatted Google Doc.

Quickest option: if your AI's response shows a download button or icon near the code block, click that first — it'll save the HTML file directly, skipping steps 1 and 2 below (you'll still need step 3 to open it). Not every AI shows this, so if you don't see one, just follow all the steps manually instead:
  1. 1 Copy only the code from the AI's reply — everything between the ```html and the closing ```, not the backticks themselves.
  2. 2 Paste it into a plain text editor — Notepad (Windows) or TextEdit (Mac, first switch to Format → Make Plain Text) — and save it as UnitPlan.html.
  3. 3 Double-click that file to open it in your web browser — it'll render as a formatted page with real tables and colours.
  4. 4 Select everything and copy it: Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C (Mac: Cmd+A then Cmd+C).
  5. 5 Open a new Google Doc and set it to Landscape first — File → Page setup → Orientation → Landscape — since this template is designed for a wide layout.
  6. 6 Paste into the doc: Ctrl+V (Mac: Cmd+V). Tables, shading, and borders should come through — you may just need to nudge column widths afterwards.
